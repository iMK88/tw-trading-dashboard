## 繁體中文

**App 名稱**（Play 30／App Store 30）：衡析－台股研究戰情室

**App Store 副標題**（30）：連回自己電腦的台股看盤與籌碼研究

**Play 簡短說明**（80）：連回你電腦上的衡析：盤中雷達、資金熱浪、當沖掃描、分點與權證追蹤、AI 盤後日報與推播。

**App Store 宣傳文字**（170）：資料與金鑰都留在你自己的電腦，手機透過你的網址連回去。摺疊手機展開時清單與走勢左右並排。沒有電腦也能用示意模式體驗所有畫面。

**完整說明**（Play 4000／App Store 4000）：

衡析是開放原始碼的台股研究戰情室。行情、籌碼與新聞由你自己電腦上的衡析，向你選擇的資料源（例如 FinMind、富果、證交所開放資料）取得；這個 App 透過你設定的網址連回電腦，讓你在手機上看盤、研究並收到推播。

【需要先在電腦安裝衡析】
衡析 Nexus 與手機版是免費的開放原始碼軟體，可在 Windows 或 Mac 執行。安裝與設定遠端存取的步驟見 GitHub：wakin61043/tw-trading-dashboard。還沒安裝的話，可以先用 App 內的「示意模式」，以合成的示意資料瀏覽所有畫面。

【主要功能】
• 今日焦點：盤前焦點新聞、本周經濟數據與財報行事曆、AI 盤後日報
• 盤勢：加權與櫃買指數走勢、資金流向、自選股、族群強弱
• 資金熱浪：全市場板塊熱浪圖，盤中每 10 秒更新
• 盤中雷達：即時看板、爆量與急拉急殺、漲跌停、型態進場提醒
• 當沖掃描：日 K、60 分 K、5 分 K 與五檔的多週期確認
• 個股：即時走勢、5 分 K、日 K、五檔與成交明細、四維評分、主力分點、大戶持股、認購權證
• 籌碼：三大法人、外資期權部位、分點追蹤、千張大戶集中度
• 自訂提醒：價位、漲跌幅、預估量達條件時推播
• 推播通知：雷達異動、自訂提醒、個股新聞、AI 日報、分點與大戶週報

【摺疊手機與平板】
展開的摺疊手機、平板或橫向時分成左右兩頁：左邊是清單，右邊是走勢與五檔。直向展開或半摺立在桌上時，改為上下兩頁。

【隱私】
App 不收集個人資料、沒有廣告、沒有追蹤。資料與金鑰都留在你的電腦。

【聲明】
衡析是研究工具。畫面上的分數、提醒、型態與 AI 文字都是研究紀錄，不是買賣訊號，也不構成投資建議。App 不提供下單或交易功能。

## English

**App name** (30): EquiLens – TW Stock Desk

**App Store subtitle** (30): Your own Taiwan stock desk

**Play short description** (80): Connect to EquiLens on your computer: radar, heatmap, chips, alerts, AI report.

**App Store promotional text** (170): Your data and API keys stay on your own computer; the app connects to it through your own address. On foldables the list and chart sit side by side. Try the demo without a computer.

**Full description** (4000):

EquiLens is an open-source research desk for Taiwan stocks. EquiLens on your own computer fetches market, chip and news data from the providers you choose (for example FinMind, Fugle and TWSE open data). This app connects to that computer through your own address, so you can follow the market, research stocks and receive push notifications on your phone.

REQUIRES EQUILENS ON YOUR COMPUTER
EquiLens Nexus and Pocket are free, open-source programs for Windows and Mac. Setup and remote-access steps are on GitHub: wakin61043/tw-trading-dashboard. Without a computer, open Demo mode in the app to browse every screen with synthetic sample data.

FEATURES
• Brief: pre-market headlines, weekly economic and earnings calendar, AI after-market report
• Market: TAIEX and TPEx charts, money flow, watchlist, group strength
• Heatmap: whole-market sector heatmap, refreshed every 10 seconds during the session
• Intraday radar: live board, volume spikes, sharp moves, limit up/down, pattern entries
• Day-trade scanner: multi-timeframe confirmation across daily, 60-minute and 5-minute bars and the order book
• Stocks: intraday chart, 5-minute and daily candles, order book and trades, four-factor score, broker branches, big holders, call warrants
• Chips: institutional flows, foreign futures/options positions, broker-branch tracking, big-holder concentration
• Custom alerts: price, change and projected volume conditions
• Push notifications: radar moves, custom alerts, stock news, AI report, broker and big-holder digests

FOLDABLES AND TABLETS
On an unfolded foldable, a tablet or in landscape, the app splits into two pages: list on the left, chart and order book on the right. Unfolded in portrait or half-folded on a desk, it splits into top and bottom pages.

PRIVACY
No personal data collection, no ads, no tracking. Your data and keys stay on your computer.

DISCLAIMER
EquiLens is a research tool. Scores, alerts, patterns and AI text are research notes, not trading signals or investment advice. The app does not place orders or trade.
